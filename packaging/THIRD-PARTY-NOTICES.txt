--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bundled VST3 plugins  (installed under vst3/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
rnnoise / noise-suppression-for-voice
  Licence : GNU General Public License version 3
  Source  : https://github.com/werman/noise-suppression-for-voice

Unmodified binary from the project's own release, loaded at runtime as a
plugin. The complete corresponding source is at the address above.

The gate, compressor, expander, limiter, EQ and gain are Wavefield's own code
(auto_cut/effects.py), ported from OBS Studio's filters - not bundled plugins.

No freeware plugins are included. Free of charge is not the same as free to
redistribute, and the ones used while developing Wavefield do not permit it.
